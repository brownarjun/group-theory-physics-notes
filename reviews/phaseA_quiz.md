## Quiz

1. State the group axioms and give the one-line proof that identity and inverses are unique.
2. Write down S₃: elements, Cayley table, all subgroups, all conjugacy classes. Which subgroups are normal?
3. Define: representation, faithful, irreducible, unitary, equivalence of representations.
4. State Schur's lemma (both parts) and prove one of them.
5. State the Schur orthogonality relations for matrix elements and for characters.
6. Compute the character table of S₃ from scratch using class sums. No looking.
7. What is the regular representation? Prove every irrep appears inside it, with multiplicity its dimension.
8. State and use the projection formula to decompose a given representation.
9. Physics: why do energy degeneracies come in multiples that equal irrep dimensions?
10. Compute the character table of S₄ from scratch (classes, class sums, eigenvectors, normalisation).
